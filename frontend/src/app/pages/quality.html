How the figures are checked

Every morning at 05:00

  1. 1

    Collected

    The source workbook and the public reference pages (governorates, public holidays) are copied into a landing area, unchanged.

  2. 2

    Loaded

    The 16 source tables are loaded into the warehouse as they are, row for row.

  3. 3

    Rebuilt and tested

    The reporting tables are rebuilt from scratch, with 43 tests along the way (unique keys, required values, links between tables, accepted values). A failing test stops every table that depends on it.

  4. 4

    Checked at the gate

    15 final checks on the reporting tables, including that no reservation was lost and that no personal data reached them. If one fails, the run is marked failed and the figures should not be presented.
